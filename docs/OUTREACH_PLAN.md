# Outreach: pitch and submission list

Goal: get InviteStudio mentioned on other sites. Search engines and AI assistants recommend what other sites say about a product, not only what the product says about itself. This is the off-site half of `docs/SEO_PLAN.md`.

**Status of this list:** I could not check each site's current submission rules from here. Treat every entry as "look up the current process first", and never pay to be listed without checking what you get. Fill in the `[brackets]` before sending.

## Rules that keep this honest

- Say only what the product does today: animated templates, event details and photos, RSVP with a spreadsheet download, a private preview, one shareable link, pay once to publish, no subscription, weddings only, no custom domains yet.
- Do not claim reviews, couple counts or awards you do not have. "New" is fine and often welcomed.
- Do not name or criticise competitors. If asked how you differ, say what you do well.
- Do not post the same text in many communities, and do not ask friends to leave fake reviews. Both get you banned and can get the site demoted.

## Prepare first (one afternoon)

| Asset | Why |
|---|---|
| 3 to 5 screenshots and a 30 to 60 second screen recording of the first screen of 3 templates, the editor and an RSVP reply | Every directory and every editor asks for them |
| The social card `assets/og-image.png` (1200x630) and a square logo | Directory listings |
| One live demo invitation people can open without signing up | Reviewers click this first |
| A short founder story: who you are, why you built it, in 3 sentences | Editors and communities respond to people |
| 2 or 3 real couples who agree to share their published invitation and a sentence about it (with written permission) | The strongest proof you can get |
| A reviewer offer, for example a free publish for bloggers who want to try it | Lets writers use it before writing about it |

## Boilerplate (use as the base for everything)

**One line (under 100 characters):**
InviteStudio builds animated wedding invitation websites with RSVP, shared as one link.

**Short (about 50 words):**
InviteStudio is a wedding invitation website builder. Couples pick an animated, mobile-first template, add their events, venues and photos, collect guest RSVPs and share one link by WhatsApp, text or email. Building and previewing are free; you pay once to publish, with no subscription.

**Medium (about 100 words):**
InviteStudio helps couples create a wedding invitation website instead of, or alongside, a printed card. Choose from 14 animated templates, from curtains that part to a short film of falling blossom. Add your names, date, event schedule, venues, travel details and photos, then preview it privately. Guests open one link on their phone and reply on the page; you see every reply in your editor and can download them as a spreadsheet. Building and previewing are free. You pay once, when you publish, and the price depends on the design and your country. There is no subscription. Weddings only, for now.

## Template 3: communities (value first)

Do not post an advert. Answer real questions, such as "how do I collect RSVPs without a spreadsheet?", with useful advice, and mention InviteStudio only where it directly helps and the rules allow it, saying you built it.

> I made a tool for exactly this, so I'm biased. [One or two sentences of genuinely useful advice that stands alone.] If it helps, it's invitestudio.co. Happy to answer questions about how the RSVP part works.

## Template 4: couples who used it

> Hi [names], congratulations again! Would you be happy for us to feature your invitation ([link]) on our site, and to add a sentence about your experience? You can say no, and you can ask us to remove it at any time. If yes, please reply "yes, you may feature our invitation" and send a sentence you are happy for us to quote.

Get the yes in writing before using anything. Do not publish names, dates or venues without it.

## Where to submit, in order

### 1. India first (your pricing, contact and likely first customers)

Wedding planning directories and magazines read by Indian couples. Look up each one's current "list your business" or "write for us" page.

- WedMeGood
- WeddingWire India
- ShaadiSaga
- WeddingSutra
- Wedding planning blogs and Instagram wedding pages focused on India (search "wedding invitation ideas India" and note who ranks)

### 2. Software and startup directories (fast, usually free to list)

- Product Hunt (plan a launch day; needs a maker account and a gallery)
- BetaList
- SaaSHub
- AlternativeTo (list InviteStudio as an alternative where it honestly fits)
- Indie Hackers (a product page and a launch post)
- G2 and Capterra (free listing; reviews come later from real customers)

### 3. Wedding sites with tool or vendor sections (international)

- Wedding publications that publish "best wedding website" and "best online invitation" roundups. Search for those titles, list the authors, and send Template 1 to the ones whose article is under 2 years old.
- Wedding forums and communities, such as the weddingplanning communities on Reddit, using Template 3 only.

### 4. Your own proof

- A "Real invitations" page on invitestudio.co once you have 2 or 3 couples who agreed.
- A short blog post per real couple's invitation, with permission.
