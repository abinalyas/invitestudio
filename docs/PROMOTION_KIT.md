# Promotion kit

Ready-to-use material for launching InviteStudio. Fill the `[brackets]` before posting.

## Rules (from `docs/OUTREACH_PLAN.md` and what the product does today)

- **Say only what is true:** animated templates, events and photos, RSVP with a spreadsheet download, private preview, one shareable link, pay once to publish (no subscription), stays online until 2 months after the event, weddings only, no custom domains yet.
- **Never use:** invented testimonials, couple counts, awards, "#1", or competitor names. The three testimonials on the home page are placeholders; do not quote them anywhere.
- **No prices in posts.** They differ by country. Say "pay once, only when you publish".
- Every link gets campaign tags (below), so `/admin` shows which post brought people.

## The message

**One line:** An animated wedding invitation your guests open on their phone, with your events, photos and RSVP in one link.

**Three points to repeat:**
1. **Free until you publish.** Build and preview as much as you like.
2. **One link.** Schedule, venues, photos and replies, all in one place, shared on WhatsApp.
3. **A first screen guests remember.** Curtains that part, falling blossom, floating lanterns, a date to scratch away.

## 1. Soft launch: first 30 to 50 couples

**WhatsApp message to someone you know who is engaged:**

> Hi [name]! I've built something for weddings and I'd love your honest opinion. It's an animated invitation you can send as one link, with your events, photos and RSVP in it. You can build and preview it free. Want to try it? I'll help if you get stuck: [link]
> If you like it, I'll give you a code so publishing is on us. [only if true]

**Message to a friend who is not engaged but knows someone:**

> Do you know anyone getting married this year? I've made a tool for animated wedding invitations. Free to build, RSVP built in. If you could pass it on I'd be grateful: [link]

**Follow-up after they publish (ask for a real testimonial):**

> Congratulations, it looks beautiful! Would you be happy to tell me in a sentence or two how it went? And may I show your invitation link and your names on our site? I'll only do it if you say yes. [Never post without a written yes.]

**Promo codes:** make one per cohort in `/admin` ("Promo codes"): who it is for, how many uses, how many days. Do not share one code publicly.

**Reel scripts (15 to 30 seconds, screen-record the phone):**

1. **"Watch this invitation open."** Record only the opening of Velvet Reveal or Lantern Night, no voice-over. Text on screen: *This is what your guests see.* End card: *Build yours free.*
2. **"From nothing to invitation."** Speed up building one invitation: choose a design, add names, add events, preview. On-screen steps: *1 Choose · 2 Add details · 3 Preview · 4 Publish.* End: *Free until you publish.*
3. **"Where do your wedding details live?"** Show a messy chat screenshot (use fake chats, never real people) then the single invitation link. Text: *One link instead of twelve threads.*
4. **"The RSVP list, without chasing."** Show the replies list and the spreadsheet download. Text: *Every reply in one place.* Use test data, never real guests.

**Stories:** poll "Which opening would you pick?" with two design screenshots, then link sticker to the winner's `/templates/<id>`.

## 10. Check before posting

- No fake reviews, counts or awards.
