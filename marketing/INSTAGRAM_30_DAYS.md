## How to post every Reel

1. Upload the clip. **Add trending audio in the app** (soft, romantic or cinematic; check that it is free to use for business accounts).
2. Put the **on-screen hook** as text for the first 2 seconds. It decides whether people keep watching.
3. Cover: the frame where the names appear.
4. Caption below, then 5 to 8 hashtags from the set at the end.
5. Reply to every comment within the hour. Comments in the first hour decide how far a Reel goes.
6. Share it to Stories with a link sticker to that design: `https://invitestudio.co/templates/<id>?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=story&utm_campaign=<design>`.
